Sleeve~ & surface-design for Hugh Featherstone album.

An unconventional but classy little design for a very special high-end recording in front of a live audience at "The Chapel" studio in Belgium, BE. Even more special: the guitar on this sleeve was custom-designed and built in a limited edition for Hugh Featherstone himself. Minus the wings, of course.
